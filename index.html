Right on Target — The DART Team Framework
Four principles guiding our dialysis & apheresis team practice
D
Direction
Clear clinical goals, patient care protocols, and shared strategic targets.
A
Alignment
Defined staff roles working cohesively to actively improve patient safety and care quality.
R
Respect
Treating all colleagues fairly, courteously, and professionally regardless of position.
T
Teamwork
Cross-functional collaboration in high-stress environments to ensure optimal patient outcomes.
